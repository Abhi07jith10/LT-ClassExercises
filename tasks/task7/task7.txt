4. Count Even Numbers
Count the total number of even numbers present in a nested list.

code:

 [12, 15, 18],
 [21, 24, 27],
 [30, 33, 36]
]

count=0
for i in numbers:
    for j in i:
        if j%2==0:
            count+=1

print(f"total even numbers is {count}")

12.Create a nested dictionary containing the price, quantity, and discount percentage of 3 products. Calculate the final price of each product after applying the discount.

products = {
 101: {"price": 1000, "quantity": 2, "discount": 10},
 102: {"price": 500, "quantity": 3, "discount": 20},
 103: {"price": 2000, "quantity": 1, "discount": 15}
}

for i in products:
    discount=products[i]["price"]*products[i]["discount"]/100
    discount_price=products[i]["price"]-discount
